Good to know

questions before you book

What if I feel awkward in photos?

That is completely normal. Lexus gives simple direction throughout the session and keeps things moving so the photographs feel natural instead of forced.

How do I choose a location?

Lexus can recommend outdoor locations based on the look, season, travel distance, and kind of session you want.

What happens after I request a date?

Lexus reviews your request, confirms the date directly with you, and sends the next planning details. The date is not final until confirmed.

Can I ask about a date that is not listed?

Absolutely. Send an inquiry through the form and include your preferred date or general timeframe.
