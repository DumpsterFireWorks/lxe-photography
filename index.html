Good to know

Before you book.

How does the $60 introductory offer work?

The first 10 booked mini sessions are $60. Once those ten sessions are filled, the regular mini-session price returns to $75.

What holds my session date?

After Lexus confirms availability, a $25 nonrefundable retainer reserves the date and applies toward the total price. The remaining balance is due by the session date.

Will my photographs be shared publicly?

Portfolio and social-media use will be discussed before the session. Any agreed permission will be clear before photographs are shared.

What if I feel awkward in photographs?

That is completely normal. Lexus uses simple direction and gentle prompts so the experience feels natural rather than stiff.

How many photographs will I receive?

Each session lists its included number of edited digital images. Additional edited images and full mini-session galleries are available as add-ons.
