Meet Lexus

Photography started at home

Photography has always been one of the ways I hold onto the moments that matter most.

As a mom of two little ones, I am constantly reminded how quickly life changes. The tiny expressions, everyday laughter, and ordinary moments can become the memories we treasure most later.

I have spent years photographing the people I love. Now I am ready to grow, learn, and capture those meaningful moments for others too. LXE Photography is the beginning of that journey.

I am currently offering complimentary portfolio-building sessions as I expand my experience and develop my style. My goal is to create a relaxed, thoughtful experience and photographs that feel genuine to you.

Lexus Erickson

Ask a question

Good to know

before you apply

Why are the sessions complimentary?

Lexus is building hands-on experience and a broader portfolio. Clients receive a planned session and edited gallery while helping her learn and grow.

Will my photos be posted publicly?

Portfolio and social-media use will be discussed before the session. Lexus will not present the arrangement as permission without first confirming the agreed terms.

What if I feel awkward in photos?

That is completely normal. Lexus gives simple direction throughout the session and keeps things relaxed instead of forcing stiff poses.

How many images will I receive?

The final number depends on the session and usable results. Lexus will explain the expected gallery and delivery timing when she confirms your booking.

Is every request automatically accepted?

No. Lexus will choose sessions that fit her current learning goals, availability, location, and portfolio needs.
