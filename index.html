Good to know

Before you book.

What holds my session date?

After Lexus confirms availability, a $25 nonrefundable retainer reserves the date and applies toward the total price. The remaining balance is due by the session date.

What happens if the weather does not cooperate?

If weather makes the session unsafe or impractical, Lexus will work with you to select a new date and the retainer will transfer to the rescheduled session.

Can I reschedule?

Contact Lexus as early as possible. Rescheduling is subject to availability, and the final terms are confirmed before the retainer is paid.

How long does editing take?

The expected turnaround is confirmed before booking because it can vary by session type and season. Rush editing is offered only when availability allows.

Will I receive RAW or unedited files?

No. The finished gallery includes Lexus’s selected and professionally edited digital photographs.

Will my photographs be shared publicly?

Portfolio and social-media use is discussed before the session. Photographs are shared only under the permission agreed with the client or parent or legal guardian.

What if I feel awkward in photographs?

That is completely normal. Lexus uses simple direction and gentle prompts so the experience feels natural rather than stiff.
