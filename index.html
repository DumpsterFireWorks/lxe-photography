Good to know

Before you apply.

Why are the sessions complimentary?

Lexus is building hands-on experience and a broader portfolio. Clients receive a planned session and edited gallery while helping her learn and grow.

Will my photographs be shared publicly?

Portfolio and social-media use will be discussed before the session. Any agreed permission will be clear before photographs are shared.

What if I feel awkward in photographs?

That is completely normal. Lexus uses simple direction and gentle prompts so the experience feels natural rather than stiff.

How many photographs will I receive?

The final gallery depends on the session and usable results. Lexus will explain expectations and delivery timing when she confirms the booking.

Is every request automatically accepted?

No. Lexus will choose sessions that fit her current learning goals, availability, location, and portfolio needs.
