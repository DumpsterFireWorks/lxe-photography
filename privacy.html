Privacy notice

LXE Photography collects only the information a visitor chooses to provide when asking about a session, such as a name, email address, phone number, preferred date, session type, and message.

How information is used

Inquiry details are used to respond, discuss availability, plan a requested session, and maintain basic booking records. LXE Photography does not sell inquiry information.

Website delivery

The website may use Cloudflare services to host the site, protect it from abuse, and deliver inquiry messages. If direct website delivery is unavailable, the form opens the visitor’s email application so the visitor can review and send the inquiry directly.

Photographs

Portfolio and social-media use is discussed separately from an inquiry. Photographs are shared publicly only under the permission agreed with the client or, for a minor, the parent or legal guardian.
